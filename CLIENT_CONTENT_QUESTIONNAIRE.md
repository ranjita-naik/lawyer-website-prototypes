# Client Content Questionnaire

Purpose: once you've chosen a design direction (or a combination of elements) from the three
prototypes, we'll need your actual details to replace every placeholder — name, biography,
photograph, credentials, practice areas, matters, contact information, and so on. This
questionnaire collects that information in one place. You don't need to answer everything at
once — the "Essential" section is enough to build a first real draft; the "Optional" section adds
depth over time.

Nothing here needs to be typed out perfectly — bullet points, voice notes, or a WhatsApp message
in your own words all work; we'll shape it into web copy together.

---

## Essential — needed to move from prototype to real first draft

**Identity**
- Preferred professional title/name as it should appear on the site (e.g. "Advocate [Full Name]," with or without post-nominals).
- A short professional photograph (headshot, ideally also one environmental/office shot). If none exists yet, we can arrange a simple photo session.
- Years in practice, and the year you were enrolled as an advocate.
- Bar Council enrolment details (state council + enrolment number), exactly as they should be displayed.
- Which courts/forums you primarily appear before (e.g. a specific High Court, District Courts, specific tribunals or arbitral institutions).

**Biography**
- A short account of your background and practice in your own words — need not be polished, we'll edit for web. If easier, answer instead: how did you come to practice in your current areas, and what do you want a new client to understand about how you work?

**Practice areas**
- Which 3–6 areas should the site lead with? (You don't need to list everything you've ever handled — just what you want to be known for and receive enquiries about.)
- For each: one or two lines on what kind of matters you actually take on within that area.

**Representative experience**
- Two or three examples of matters you're comfortable describing in anonymized/general terms (no client names, no confidential details, no outcome claims) — enough to illustrate the kind of work you do.

**Contact and office**
- Office address(es) — full postal address for each location, if more than one.
- Phone number(s) and email address(es) you want published.
- Preferred way for prospective clients to reach you first (phone, email, a contact form, WhatsApp, or by appointment only).
- Office hours, if you want them listed.

**Pages**
- Beyond Home, About, Practice Areas, Experience, Contact, and the Disclaimer page — is there anything you specifically want included or specifically want left out?

---

## Optional — adds depth once the essentials are in place

**Credentials and standing**
- Education (degrees, institutions, years).
- Any additional qualifications, certifications, or specialized training.
- Professional memberships (bar associations, arbitration institutions, etc.).
- Languages you practice in.

**Thought leadership**
- Any articles, papers, or notes you've written (or would like to write) that could become "Insights" content.
- Any speaking engagements, panels, or lectures worth mentioning.

**Working style**
- How would you describe your approach to a first conversation with a prospective client? Is there anything you want them to know before reaching out (e.g. what to bring, what it does/doesn't commit them to)?
- Any frequently asked procedural questions you'd like addressed on the site (e.g. "What happens after I send an enquiry?").

**Tone and references**
- Any websites — legal or otherwise — you like or dislike, and why (even a one-line reaction is useful: "too busy," "too cold," "I like how calm this feels").
- Words you'd want a visitor to use to describe the site after visiting it.
- Anything you specifically do NOT want published (a past matter, a topic, a personal detail, a photo style, etc.).

**Legal/compliance**
- Any disclaimer language your Bar Council association or personal practice requires beyond the general legal-information disclaimer we've drafted (this should ultimately be reviewed by you and, if useful, independently for compliance with applicable Bar Council of India advertising/solicitation rules).
- Any restrictions you're aware of on what an advocate's website in India may or may not state.

---

*This questionnaire accompanies three prototype design presentations. It does not need to be
completed before reviewing the prototypes — it's for the next stage, once a direction is chosen.*
