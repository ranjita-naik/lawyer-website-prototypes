Biography

Arjun Mehta is an independent advocate based in Mumbai, in practice since 2011. His work spans commercial, civil and dispute-resolution matters, combining advisory work for businesses with representation before courts and arbitral tribunals.

He began in general civil litigation and, after completing an LL.M. in Commercial Law, moved progressively toward commercial and arbitration-focused work. That route informs how he reads a commercial problem: with attention to how a clause is likely to be argued if it is ever tested, and not only to how it reads on the day it is signed.

He practises without a large associate team and works directly with the clients he advises. Each matter is taken on its own terms — attentive, direct and specific to the facts — rather than routed through a standard process.

He appears before the Bombay High Court and the City Civil Court, Mumbai, and acts as counsel in institutional arbitration proceedings. He works in English, Hindi and Marathi.
