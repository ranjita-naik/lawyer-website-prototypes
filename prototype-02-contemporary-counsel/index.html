Areas of practice

Four areas of concentrated advisory and disputes work.

The practice is deliberately narrow. Each area draws on the same commercial grounding, so advice given at the drafting stage anticipates how a term will be argued if it is ever tested.

Corporate Advisory

Advising founders and businesses on commercial contracts, governance matters and the day-to-day legal questions that arise in running a company.

Commercial Disputes

Advising and representing businesses in disputes arising from contracts, transactions and commercial relationships, with an emphasis on resolving matters efficiently wherever possible.

Arbitration and Mediation

Acting as counsel in institutional and ad-hoc arbitration proceedings, and advising parties considering mediation as a route to resolving disputes outside of court.

Employment Matters

Advising employers and employees on employment contracts, workplace policies and disputes arising from the employment relationship.

How the practice works

A small practice, run so that the person advising you is the person doing the work.

Direct involvement

Matters are handled by Arjun Mehta directly. There is no large associate team between the client and the work, and no handover after the first meeting.

Built on the facts

Advice is shaped by the commercial context of the particular matter rather than run through a standard template, which matters most when a contract is likely to be tested.

One practice, several forums

Advisory work, proceedings before the Bombay High Court and the City Civil Court, and institutional arbitration are handled within the same practice.
