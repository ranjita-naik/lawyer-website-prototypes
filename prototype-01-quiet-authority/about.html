Arjun Mehta, Advocate.

Fourteen years of practice in Mumbai, across commercial disputes, civil litigation, arbitration and constitutional matters.

Biography

Arjun Mehta has practised as an advocate in Mumbai since 2011. He began in general civil litigation — the ordinary run of contract, property and civil wrong matters that teach a lawyer how a case actually behaves — and moved gradually toward commercial disputes and arbitration as the practice settled.

An LL.M. in Commercial Law, completed in 2014, gave that shift its shape. Since establishing independent chambers at Fort in 2017 he has appeared before the Bombay High Court, the City Civil Court at Mumbai and institutional arbitral tribunals, and has advised small and mid-sized businesses on the commercial contracts that sit behind most of those disputes.

He works directly with the people he acts for, rather than through a large associate team. It makes for a smaller practice and, he would say, a more attentive one: each matter read closely, advised on directly, and answered plainly — whatever the answer happens to be.

Approach

Three working habits.

Attentive

Matters are handled by the advocate the client instructed. There is no layer between the person with the problem and the person advising on it.

Direct

Advice is given in plain terms, including when the plain terms are unwelcome. A client is better served by an early candid view of a weak position than by a late one.

Matter-specific

No two disputes want the same treatment. Strategy follows the facts, the forum and the client's own appetite for time and cost — not a standard template.
