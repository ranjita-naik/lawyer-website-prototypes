Professional timeline

The practice widened slowly and in one direction: from general civil litigation toward commercial disputes, arbitration and advisory work.

2011

Enrolled as an Advocate; began practice in civil and commercial litigation, Mumbai.

2014

Completed LL.M. (Commercial Law); began taking on arbitration-related matters.

2017

Established independent chambers in Fort, Mumbai.

2020

Began regularly appearing in institutional arbitration proceedings.

2023

Expanded advisory work with small and mid-sized businesses on commercial contracts.
