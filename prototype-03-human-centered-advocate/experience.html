Why settlement is raised early.

In family, succession and property matters, a resolution that the parties have shaped themselves is often more durable than one imposed on them — and it tends to leave relationships intact where an order rarely does. That is why mediation is discussed at the start of a matter rather than after a suit has been filed. It is not right for every dispute, and where it is not, it will not be recommended.
