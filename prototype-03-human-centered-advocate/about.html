Professional profile

Arjun Mehta

Advocate, in independent practice in Mumbai since 2011.

Arjun Mehta has practised as an advocate in Mumbai since 2011. He works on his own rather than through a large team of associates, which means the person you speak with at the first meeting is the person who stays with the matter.

His practice began in general civil litigation. An LL.M. in Commercial Law, completed in 2014, drew him toward commercial and dispute-resolution work, and he has since appeared regularly in institutional arbitration alongside a continuing court practice at the Bombay High Court and the City Civil Court, Mumbai.

A good deal of what reaches these chambers is not purely commercial. Property, succession and family matters carry long histories, and usually involve people who will still have to speak to one another afterwards. Where a matter can be settled by agreement, he says so early; where it cannot, he prepares it properly.

He describes his approach as attentive, direct and specific to the matter at hand. He works in English, Hindi and Marathi.

How this office works

Four things worth knowing before you write in.

  • You will deal with the advocate directly

    Correspondence, meetings and appearances are handled by Arjun Mehta himself. Fewer matters are taken on as a result, and each one is read closely.

  • Settlement is discussed before litigation

    In family, succession and property matters especially, mediation or negotiation is raised as an option at the outset — not as a fallback after a suit has been filed. If it is unlikely to work in your matter, that is said too.

  • Plain language, then the legal language

    Pleadings, notices and orders are explained in ordinary words first. You should be able to describe your own matter accurately to your family after the meeting.

  • No promises about outcomes

    No advocate can tell you how a matter will end. What can be set out honestly is the likely process, the stages involved and the work each stage requires.
