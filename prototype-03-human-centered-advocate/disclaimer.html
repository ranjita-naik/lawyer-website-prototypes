Disclaimer

Two notices apply to this website. The first concerns the fact that this is a design prototype. The second is the standard legal information notice that applies to an advocate’s website.

Notice one

Prototype disclaimer

This website is a design prototype prepared for review purposes only. The name, photograph, credentials, biography, matters, articles and contact details shown are fictional placeholders created solely to demonstrate the design and structure of the website. They do not describe a real person, and should not be relied upon for any purpose.

Notice two

Legal information disclaimer

The information presented on this website is for general informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Viewing this website or contacting the advocate does not, by itself, create an advocate-client relationship.

What is placeholder content on this site

So that nothing on this prototype is mistaken for fact, the following items are invented for demonstration and should all be replaced before any website of this kind is published.

Identity and photograph
“Arjun Mehta” is a fictional name. The portrait used throughout the site is a stock photograph of a model, licensed from Unsplash and used purely as a stand-in likeness. It is not a photograph of any advocate.
Credentials and enrolment
The enrolment number MAH/1284/2011, the bar association membership and the educational qualifications shown are illustrative and do not correspond to any real record.
Illustrative matters
Every matter described on the experience page is an anonymized composite written for this prototype. Each carries the caption: Illustrative composite example, anonymized for demonstration purposes only. Not an actual client matter and not a representation of case outcome.
Articles
Article titles and text were written as placeholder content to demonstrate layout. They are general in nature, are not legal advice, and are not tailored to any situation.
Contact details
The address at Ruby House, the telephone number +91 22 0000 0000 and the email address on the “.example” domain are all placeholders. None of them reaches anyone. The enquiry form does not submit any data.
Imagery
All photographs on this prototype are licensed from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and are temporary placeholders. Portrait photography by Ratul Puri; architectural photography by Abizer Shihorwala; texture photography by Annie Spratt and Kiwihug.

No solicitation

Nothing on this website is intended to advertise or solicit work. It is intended only to provide general information about an advocate’s areas of practice and how the office may be contacted. No claim is made about the quality of the services offered, and no comparison is drawn with any other advocate or firm.
