Before you write

Three things that make a first reply more useful.

  • Say what has happened, in the order it happened.
  • Mention any date that is already fixed or running.
  • Say what you would like the outcome to be, even if it seems unlikely.

What happens next

After you write in.

  1. 1

    A reply, either way

    You will hear back whether or not the chambers can take the matter on. If it cannot, that is said directly rather than left unanswered.

  2. 2

    A first conversation

    By appointment at Fort, or by telephone. Bring the papers you have, in whatever state they are in.

  3. 3

    The options, in plain words

    Negotiation, mediation or litigation, with what each would involve in your matter. No outcome can be promised at any stage.
